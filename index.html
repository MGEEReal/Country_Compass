How to play

With each correct guess, a random eligible city becomes the new center and the arc shifts to a different random direction. The arc narrows through 180°, 150°, 120°, 90°, 45°, and 27°.

N North
E East
S South
W West

The arc moves around the compass each level. Its number is the full width of the allowed angle.

Get one guess right at every width to win. A wrong city starts the challenge over at 180°. City and country suggestions appear as you type; tap one on a phone or use the arrow keys and Enter.

Capital coordinates: GeoNames and mledoze/countries.
